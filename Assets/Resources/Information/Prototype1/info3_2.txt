Information from Old Expedition Records
Historical maps place the starting area southwest of a large Forest. An old Bridge beyond
the Forest provides access to the central region.
A major mountain range lies east of this central region. The Treasure is believed to lie
beyond the eastern side of the Mountains, northeast of the Abandoned Village and
southeast of the Misty Marsh.
Ruins stand against the western side of the Mountains.
Several drawings of the Ruins contain a distinctive carved symbol. The same symbol also
appears in an expedition sketch made on the eastern side of the mountain range. No route
connecting these locations is shown.
One surviving journal contains the sentence:
“The peaks remained above us, yet when daylight returned they stood behind
us.”
The pages immediately before this sentence are missing.
North of the Mountains stands an old Warding Waystone.
Records also mention a Monster Nest further northeast, although they give little information
about how the creatures detect travellers or how far their territory extends.
A large Misty Marsh lies north of the Monster Nest. One expedition record describes
travellers entering its western side and later leaving the eastern edge close to the Treasure
region.
Another recognised route goes around the southern edge of the Mountains, passes through
an Abandoned Village and then continues northeast toward the Treasure. It is described as
reliable but relatively long.
