Knowledge Collected from Local Hunters
Travellers entering from the southwest normally pass through a large Forest before reaching
an old Bridge across the river.
Beyond the Bridge, a large mountain range separates the central region from the eastern
lands where the Treasure is believed to be hidden.
Ruins stand somewhere near the western side of the Mountains. Local stories describe
travellers reaching the eastern side of the range without crossing the high ridges, although
nobody agrees on how they did it.
North of the Mountains lies a Monster Nest. The creatures are territorial and mainly detect
travellers by sight, especially in open terrain.
An ancient Warding Waystone stands southwest of the nesting area. Hunters use it as a
warning that they are approaching dangerous territory. Travelling further northeast can bring
travellers into sight of the creatures.
A large Misty Marsh lies around the northern side of the nesting grounds. Its fog is extremely
dense, and the monsters appear unable to see far into it.
The exact route through the Marsh is unfamiliar to you.
A longer route also passes around the southern side of the Mountains. It avoids the Monster
Nest and eventually reaches an Abandoned Village before continuing east.
The Treasure lies somewhere beyond the mountain range.

Local saying
“In open land they see you. In the white mist, even their eyes are useless.”
