Notes from a Regional Survey
The expedition begins in the southwest. A large forest lies between the starting area and the
river. Travelling around it is impractical, so expeditions normally pass through it.
Near the northeastern edge of the Forest stands an old stone Bridge, providing the main
crossing into the central region.
A large mountain range lies east of the Bridge. Broken Ruins stand close to the western
edge of the Mountains. Parts of the Ruins continue underground and disappear beneath the
mountain slope, although their purpose is unknown.
North of the Mountains stands an ancient Warding Waystone. Its purpose is not recorded.
A Monster Nest has been reported somewhere northeast of the Waystone. The exact size of
the creatures' territory is unclear.
A large Misty Marsh lies north of the Monster Nest. It bends around the northern side of the
nesting area and continues eastward.
The Treasure is believed to lie east of the Mountains and southeast of the Marsh.
Travellers can also head south from the Bridge and follow the lower edge of the Mountains.
This route reaches an Abandoned Village, with the Treasure lying further northeast.
