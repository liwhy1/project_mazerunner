The Archivist
From a very young age, everyone in the province of Moldrovia is told the same story about
“the lost treasure of the old king.” It’s said that its contents consist of magical items of
unimaginable value and power.
The exact location of the treasure remains a mystery. But it is known that it resides
somewhere in the valley near the north coast of the province. Very few adventurers that
have ever entered the valley to try to find this treasure have ever come back. And none have
been able to find it…..
You are a librarian in the province’s largest and oldest library. Yesterday while cleaning the
shelves, you found a really tiny old book with no title on the cover. When you turned to the
first page, the book turned out to be the old king’s jester’s log from the days of the king's
perishment. You wrote down some of the things you thought could be helpful to someone
trying to find the king’s treasure. That night you dream about finding the lost treasure no one
else has even been able to find. That morning you make a decision, “I am going to try and
find the lost treasure.” You fill your bag with maps about the way to the valley and you start
your journey to find the lost treasure.
At the large road crossing before the valley you meet 2 other travelers. After talking a bit you
all discover that the 3 of you are all looking for the lost treasure. You decide to put all the
information you have gathered together to be able to map out the valley and find a safe and
efficient route to the lost treasure.
