<b><i>The valley and its reports</i></b>
<b>At Split Peak yesterday</b>
“A traveller saw fresh bootprints entering the old mountain passage. A cold draught came from
inside, but the bend concealed what lay beyond.”

<b>Near the Bridge yesterday</b>
“From the opposite bank, I saw black thorns beside the Waystone approach. I turned away
without crossing to inspect the road.”

<b>Ordinary roads</b>
These records are shared by all three travellers. Ordinary routes are open: the keeper-assisted
lift, slow Marsh track, outer mountain road and Nest road. No hidden combat costs apply. One
point is one hour for the whole party.
North is up; the river flows northwest to southeast. Start, Forest and Ruins are on the northeast
bank. Forest is northwest of Start; Ruins southwest of Forest. The Chainlift is southwest of
Ruins and crosses to the southwest bank; Treasure is west and slightly south of its landing.
Marsh is south and slightly west of Start. Marsh to Waystone crosses the river; Waystone is
west and slightly north of Marsh. Nest is south of Waystone; Treasure northwest of Nest.
Village is northwest of Waystone and southeast of Treasure.
Mountains marks the upstream crossing at the river’s northwest end. The Start road passes
north of Forest without joining it and crosses near Mountains. Treasure is south and somewhat
west of Mountains. The tunnel is a feature within Mountains, not a new landmark.
